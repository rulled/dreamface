# Promo Video Script

## Duration
20 to 30 seconds

## Shot list

1. Open a DreamFace page with available character cards.
   - Caption: `Scan DreamFace avatars and build groups fast`

2. Open the extension popup and show one or two groups with multiple audio files.
   - Caption: `Attach many audio files at once`

3. Toggle `600 sec` mode and `overlap`.
   - Caption: `Handle short and long audio automatically`

4. Start the queue and show the live monitor.
   - Caption: `Track progress while the queue runs`

5. Switch to Creations and show ready results.
   - Caption: `Check and download ready results from Creations`

## Voiceover draft

DreamFace Batch Assistant helps you run large DreamFace audio batches with fewer manual steps. Scan available characters, build grouped queues, process audio locally in the browser, and follow progress from a compact monitor. When tasks finish, move to Creations and download ready results in bulk.
