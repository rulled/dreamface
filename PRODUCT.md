# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operators running bulk DreamFace avatar-video generation across multiple saved accounts. They need to submit a batch, leave it processing, and quickly understand whether results are still generating, downloading, completed, or failed.

## Product Purpose

Prepare audio locally, distribute DreamFace jobs across accounts, track each result, and download completed videos automatically. Success means the operator can understand the current state and remaining work without reading technical logs.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, direct, utilitarian. Use as little text as possible while keeping state and actions unambiguous.

## Anti-references

No debug-dashboard aesthetic, decorative analytics, legacy preprocessing statistics after submission, ambiguous details drawers, redundant queue maps, or card-heavy layouts.

## Design Principles

- Show workflow state, not implementation details.
- Keep every count tied to a clear stage.
- Make account ownership and failures visible.
- Prefer one concise status over several competing summaries.
- Show actions only when they are useful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, clear labels, keyboard-compatible controls, and polite live status announcements.
